City Medical Clinic — Policies

Cancellation policy
Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment. You can cancel by phone during business hours. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice, and missed appointments with no call, are marked as a late cancel. The first late cancel in a calendar year is waived. Each additional late cancel in that year has a 50 dollar fee, billed to the patient and not to insurance.

Arrival policy
If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, the visit may be shortened or rescheduled so the next patient is not delayed. A late arrival that cannot be seen counts as a late cancel under the cancellation policy.

Refunds and billing
Copays are collected at check-in. If a visit is canceled at least 24 hours ahead, no fee is charged. The late-cancel fee is refundable only when the clinic canceled the visit or when a documented emergency prevented attendance. Insurance claims are filed as a courtesy. Questions about a statement go to billing option 3, or to billing@citymedical.example. Payment plans are available for balances over 200 dollars.

New patient paperwork
Registration forms must be finished before the clinician can start the visit. Incomplete paperwork can delay or reschedule the appointment. Guardians must attend visits for patients under 18, unless a signed consent is already on file.

Privacy
Staff will not discuss diagnoses, test results, or appointments with anyone except the patient, unless a current release of information is on file. Voicemail messages left by the clinic state only the clinic name and a callback number.

After-hours calls
The main line has an after-hours message with instructions for urgent concerns. Nurses do not take clinical calls overnight. For a medical emergency, call 911. Prescription refill requests left after hours are reviewed the next business morning.

Forms and letters
Work excuses, FMLA paperwork, and disability forms require an office visit if you have not been seen for that condition in the last six months. Completed forms are ready in five to seven business days. There is a 25 dollar form fee when the form is longer than one page.

Conduct
Abusive language toward staff ends the call or the visit. The clinic may dismiss a patient from the practice after a written warning for repeated no-shows or abusive behavior. Dismissal letters explain how to obtain records.
