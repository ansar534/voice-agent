City Medical Clinic — Services

Primary care visits
We provide routine physicals, chronic-condition follow-up (such as high blood pressure and diabetes checks), and sick visits for colds, flu, sore throat, ear pain, and similar everyday problems. Annual wellness visits for Medicare patients are scheduled as a dedicated appointment, not added onto a sick visit.

Preventive care
Immunizations for adults and children, blood pressure checks, and routine screening labs ordered by your clinician are available on site. Flu shots are offered each fall by appointment. Travel vaccines are not given here; we refer those to the county travel clinic.

Laboratory
A basic blood-draw station is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 3:30 PM. Fasting labs should be booked before 10:00 AM. Results are released to the patient portal after the ordering clinician reviews them, usually within three business days for routine tests. The clinic does not draw labs for outside orders that were not placed by our clinicians.

Pediatrics
We see children from age 2 through 17 for well-child checks and minor illness. Infants under age 2 are referred to a pediatric practice. School and sports physical forms are completed during a scheduled well visit, not as a same-day add-on.

Women's health
Annual well-woman exams and routine Pap screening are available with our clinicians. Obstetric care and pregnancy visits are referred to a partner obstetrics practice. We do not provide labor and delivery services.

Minor procedures
We perform simple suture removal, ear irrigation, and basic skin-tag removal when a clinician has already evaluated the concern. We do not set fractures, drain large abscesses, or provide IV fluids. Those needs are directed to urgent care or the emergency department.

Referrals and imaging
Specialist referrals are placed after a clinic visit when your plan requires one. We do not have an on-site X-ray or MRI. Imaging is scheduled at the hospital outpatient center two blocks north. The referral coordinator calls you within three business days after the order is signed.

What we do not treat on site
We are not an emergency department. We do not manage chest pain, stroke symptoms, major trauma, active labor, or psychiatric emergencies. Staff will direct those callers to call 911.
