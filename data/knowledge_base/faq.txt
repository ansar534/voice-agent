City Medical Clinic — Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the clinic located?
City Medical Clinic is at 1200 Main Street, Suite 200. The entrance is on the Main Street side of the building, next to the pharmacy. Visitor parking is in the lot behind the building. The first two hours are free with a validated ticket from the front desk.

What are your hours?
Monday through Friday we are open from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Saturday hours are 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM for urgent same-week visits only. The clinic is closed on Sundays and on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day.

How do I reach the clinic by phone?
Call the main line at (555) 014-2200. For prescription refill requests already on file, choose option 2. For billing questions, choose option 3. Medical advice is not given over email.

Do you accept walk-ins?
We do not accept walk-ins for routine visits. Same-day sick appointments are released each weekday at 7:30 AM and can be booked by phone or through the receptionist. If you have chest pain, trouble breathing, severe bleeding, or signs of a stroke, call 911 instead of the clinic.

What should I bring to my first visit?
Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications including dose, and any referral your plan requires. New patients should arrive 20 minutes early to complete registration. Established patients should arrive 10 minutes early.

Which insurance plans do you accept?
We accept most major commercial plans, Medicare, and the state Medicaid plan. Coverage for a specific visit depends on your policy. The front desk can confirm whether a plan is in network before you book. We do not bill auto insurance or workers' compensation directly; those visits are self-pay unless a written authorization is already on file.

How do prescription refills work?
Request refills through your pharmacy or by calling option 2 at least two business days before you run out. Controlled medications are not refilled without an office visit. The clinician reviews refill requests on business days only.

Is there a patient portal?
Yes. After your first visit you can message the care team, see upcoming appointments, and view after-visit summaries in the patient portal. Portal messages are answered within two business days. They are not monitored for emergencies.

Do you offer telehealth?
Established patients can book a video visit for follow-up of stable conditions, medication questions, and lab-result reviews. Video visits are not used for new injuries, breathing trouble, or anything that needs a hands-on exam. A staff member emails the video link the morning of the appointment.
